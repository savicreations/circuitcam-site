CircuitCam

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

CircuitCam is a live camera app for creating processed, circuit-bent photos and videos.

Camera

CircuitCam accesses your device camera to provide a live preview and capture photos and videos.

Microphone

If you grant microphone permission, CircuitCam uses your device microphone to record audio with videos. You may deny microphone permission and continue recording silent videos.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos created with CircuitCam are processed on your device and saved to your device's media library. CircuitCam may temporarily store media files on your device while they are being processed or saved.

Data collection and sharing

CircuitCam does not require an account. CircuitCam does not upload your photos, videos, or microphone recordings to our servers. CircuitCam does not sell personal information and does not use advertising or analytics services to track users.

Data deletion

CircuitCam does not maintain an online account or server-side copy of your media. Photos and videos saved to your device can be deleted using your device's Photos or Gallery app.

Permissions

You may revoke CircuitCam's camera or microphone permissions at any time through your device settings. Features requiring those permissions may no longer function.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if CircuitCam's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact savicreationsny@gmail.com.
